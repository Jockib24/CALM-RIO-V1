Languages: The official legal version of this document is in French. The English text below is a summary provided for convenience. In case of any discrepancy, the French version shall prevail.

Last updated: July 2026

1. Introduction

Calm Rio takes the protection of your personal data seriously. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our website calm-rio.com.

2. Data Collected

We collect the following data when you use our contact form or make a booking:

  • First and last name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Booking information (dates, number of guests, property selected)
  • Browsing data (analytics cookies)

3. Purpose of Processing

Your data is used to:

  • Respond to your booking enquiries and contact you
  • Manage your stay (sending practical information, invoices)
  • Improve our website and services (anonymised analytics)
  • Comply with our legal and tax obligations

4. Legal Basis

Data processing is based on your consent (contact form) and on the performance of a contract (booking management), in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

5. Retention Period

Your data is kept for the duration necessary to manage your booking, then archived in accordance with legal obligations (5 years for tax and accounting records). Contact data not associated with a booking is retained for 3 years.

6. Cookies

This site uses only technical cookies essential for operation and anonymised analytics cookies (via GitHub Pages). No advertising or third-party tracking cookies are used. You can configure your cookie preferences in your browser settings.

7. Your Rights

Under the GDPR, you have the following rights:

  • Right to access, rectify, and erase your data
  • Right to restrict and object to processing
  • Right to data portability
  • Right to withdraw consent at any time

To exercise your rights, contact us at: contact@calm-rio.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Security

We implement all appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Payments are processed via Stripe, a PCI DSS Level 1 certified platform.

9. Data Transfers

Your data is hosted in France and within the European Union. No transfers outside the EU are carried out.

10. Contact

For any questions regarding this policy or the protection of your data:
Email: contact@calm-rio.com
You may also contact the CNIL (French data protection authority): www.cnil.fr

11. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated at any time. The date of the last revision is shown at the top of this page. We encourage you to review it periodically.
