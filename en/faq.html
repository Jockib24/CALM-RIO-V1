Booking & Payment

You can contact us via the contact form, by email at contact@calm-rio.com or by phone. We will respond within 24 hours to confirm availability and send you a secure Stripe payment link. Your booking is confirmed once the deposit (30%) has been received.
We accept card payments (Visa, Mastercard) via Stripe, as well as bank transfers. All payments are secure and your banking details are never stored.
Our cancellation policy is as follows: free cancellation up to 30 days before arrival. Between 30 and 14 days: 50% refunded. Less than 14 days: no refund. In cases of force majeure, we review each situation individually.
Yes, the nightly rate includes accommodation and utilities (water, electricity, WiFi). Tourist taxes (local visitor's tax, approx. €1/person/night) and the security deposit are extra. End-of-stay cleaning may be included or optional depending on the property — we will confirm at the time of booking.

Arrival & Stay

Check-in is generally between 4 PM and 8 PM. Check-out is before 10 AM or 11 AM depending on the property (indicated on each property page). If you have a late or early arrival, please contact us to arrange a suitable handover.
It depends on the property. The Saint-Trojan Villa accepts pets (please enquire about conditions). The Royan Apartment and Saint-Trojan House do not accept pets. Please contact us before booking if you are travelling with an animal.
Yes, all our properties welcome children. For the Saint-Trojan Villa, baby equipment (cot, high chair) can be provided upon request. Children are counted in the total number of guests.
Yes, all our properties have free high-speed WiFi suitable for remote working (video calls, streaming).

On Location

Yes. The Saint-Trojan Villa has free private parking with an EV/hybrid charging point. The Royan Apartment and Saint-Trojan House have free public parking nearby.
Yes! Several bicycle rental shops are located in Saint-Trojan-les-Bains (a 5-minute walk from the House and a short cycle from the Villa). We can recommend the best places. The Île d'Oléron is flat and perfect for cycling.
The covered market in Saint-Trojan (Wednesday and Saturday mornings) is a must-visit for fresh local produce. Supermarkets (Super U, Intermarché) are a 5-minute drive away. In Royan, the large covered market is open daily.
Grande Plage de Saint-Trojan (supervised swimming), Gatseau beach (surfing and paddleboarding), and Rémigeasse beach (wild and unspoilt). All accessible by bicycle from our properties.
