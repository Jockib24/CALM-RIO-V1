Nature & Outdoors

Between sea and forest

Imagine cycling through the Saint-Trojan forest, the scent of maritime pines mingling with the Atlantic spray. Île d'Oléron is a paradise for cyclists, with over 130 km of cycle paths winding between salt marshes, oyster parks and wild beaches.

In Royan, the Grande Conche beach stretches 2.6 km of fine sand, just 100 metres from our apartment. The famous Marennes-Oléron oysters and pineau des Charentes await you at the covered market.

Local Guide — The Beauty Coast

From the beaches of Grande Conche to the forests of Île d'Oléron, explore the treasures of the Atlantic coast.

Beaches

Grande Conche, Gatseau beach, Maumusson beach… Swimming, relaxing and unforgettable sunsets.

Cycling

Île d'Oléron, a cycling paradise with over 130 km of cycle paths through forests, marshes and villages.

Gastronomy

Marennes-Oléron oysters, fresh fish from the port, pineau des Charentes, galettes charentaises… An exceptional terroir.

Culture & Heritage

Royan central market, Notre-Dame church, Fort Boyard, Saint-Trojan's Little Train, festivals.
