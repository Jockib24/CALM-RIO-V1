OUR FAVOURITES

Discover the coast differently

BEACHES

Oléron Beaches

La Grande Plage de Saint-Trojan, Gatseau beach (surfing), Plage de la Rémigeasse. Reachable by bike from your rental. Clear water, fine sand, preserved dunes.

RESTAURANTS

Where to eat

Saint-Trojan covered market (oysters, seafood). Chez Laurent (grilled fish). Crêperie La Galettière. Gatseau beach huts.

ACTIVITIES

Must-do activities

Oléron cycle path — 120 km. Walk in the state forest. Kayaking in the straits. Chassiron Lighthouse. Île d'Oléron bridge. Visit the Château-d'Oléron citadel.

HIGHLIGHTS

Royan & surroundings

Royan Market

Royan's magnificent covered market — beautiful metal halls. Fresh produce, basin oysters, cheeses, local wines. Every morning.

Royan Port

Stroll along the marina, restaurants by the water, ferries across the Gironde estuary. Departure for Verdon-sur-Mer.

Coubre Forest

Vast pine forest between Royan and the Coubre point. Hiking, mountain biking, and the famous Customs Officers' path along the ocean.

Chassiron Lighthouse

At the northern tip of Oléron. 224 steps, 360° view over the ocean and the Antioche strait. Remarkable gardens at the foot of the lighthouse.

PRACTICAL TIPS

Enjoy a stress-free stay

Getting around

Cycling is the best way to discover Oléron. Bike rentals in Saint-Trojan. It's all flat!

Shopping

Local markets: Saint-Trojan (Wednesday, Saturday), Royan (daily). Supermarkets 5 min by car.

Tourist Office

Saint-Trojan-les-Bains and Royan tourist offices. Activity programmes, maps, advice.
