Description

Fully renovated village house in a quiet area, with 3 bedrooms including one on the ground floor, a very bright and pleasant living room with a fireplace. The house features a lovely private courtyard with garden furniture and parasol, perfect for sunny breakfasts or outdoor meals.

Walking distance to shops, a short bike ride from the beach and forest — the location is ideal for exploring the Île d'Oléron. Saint-Trojan's covered market (fresh produce, oysters, fish) is just 5 minutes on foot.

3 bedrooms: 1 with double bed 160 (ground floor), 2 with double beds 140 (upstairs). Bathroom with shower, separate WC. Fitted kitchen open to the living room: gas hob, fridge/freezer, washing machine, microwave.
