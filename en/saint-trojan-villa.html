Description

A brand-new house in the Oléronais style, blending the island's Charentais character with contemporary design. Very bright, generous volumes, ideally situated between sea and forest.

110 m² on a tree-filled, enclosed garden with a 50 m² south-facing wooden terrace (garden furniture, parasol) and a 17 m² covered terrace (teak table). BBQ. Large living/dining room with high ceilings, French windows opening onto the terraces. Open-plan fitted kitchen: dishwasher, fridge, oven, microwave, induction hob. Utility room with washing machine, tumble dryer and freezer.

3 bedrooms of 12 m² each: 2 with double beds (160 cm), 1 with two single beds (80 cm). Bathroom with walk-in shower, separate WC. Linens and towels provided. Electric/hybrid vehicle charging point (extra charge based on meter reading). Desk and HDMI screen for remote work.
